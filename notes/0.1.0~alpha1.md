**First alpha.** folio compiles real papers, but expect rough edges and breaking changes before 0.1.0.

**Source and licences.** This release's source tarball also contains the complete source of **slimtex** (GPL-2.0-or-later), vendored at revision `f3e776905e582c98e9683bb770f3f80279eade40` (`vendor/slimtex-*`). folio 0.1.0-alpha.1 still links slimtex's file-lookup, font and image crates. The slimtex dependency is being removed in 0.1.0-alpha.2.

The bundled TeX data files are unmodified TeX Live 2023 files under their own licences (LPPL, GPL, OFL, GUST, Knuth and others). They are listed in `/usr/share/doc/folio/copyright` and in `dist/manifest.tsv` in the source tarball; `TEXLIVE-DATA.txt` there says how to obtain them.
